## Description

macOS mirror of #0183. Run/adapt the settings/moderation/account coverage on **macOS**: Settings opens and lists its rows, Account settings renders, and the Moderation screen shows mutes/blocks lists + entry points — asserting rendering/reachability only (no destructive or persistent-state actions). Account for the macOS settings presentation (Settings may be a separate window / `Settings` scene on the Mac rather than a pushed screen).

## Scope

- Verify on `platform=macOS`, reusing `settings-screen` / `account-settings-screen` / `moderation-screen` / `moderation-*` identifiers from #0183.
- The #0183 suite navigated via the own-profile menu because the `openSettings`/`openModeration` intents are unreliable (see #0185); the macOS shell may surface Settings differently (menu bar / Settings window) — adapt the navigation accordingly.
- Do NOT exercise destructive controls (sign out, deactivate, password/handle change, submitting reports, blocking real users).

## Verification
